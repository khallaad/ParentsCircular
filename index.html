Keeping Our Students Safe and Cool in the Heat

A circular on the El Niño heat for parents and guardians

Date: 24 October 2026To: All Parents and GuardiansFrom: Head of School

Dear Parents and Guardians, Assalamu alaykum wa rahmatuLLAHI wa barakatuH.

There have been ongoing global and regional warnings regarding a strong El Niño climate pattern. Forecasters expect this pattern to cause higher-than-normal temperatures through the remainder of this school term and into early 2027.

The safety and comfort of your child is our priority, and to ensure they stay healthy and active, the school will be taking proactive steps to manage the heat.

What the school is doing

Cool drinking water will be made available across the compound, and students will carry their water bottles with them at all times.
Classrooms and dormitories will be kept well ventilated, with fans serviced and working.
Assembly will only be on Monday.
Staff have been informed to recognise heat illness, and the sick bay is stocked with oral rehydration salts.
Boarders will have extra rest time.

What we ask of you

Send your child with a named water bottle every day.
Make sure your child's meals are cooked and packed well.
Choose light, breathable underclothes for your children.
Let the school know beforehand if your child is unwell.
Senior students should not come to school in blazers, and ties should be worn loose and relaxed.
These are precautionary measures. We will do our best to safeguard our children in this heat period.

Any change to school hours or activities will be communicated through the usual channels. Thank you for your continued partnership. May Allah protect and keep our children well.
